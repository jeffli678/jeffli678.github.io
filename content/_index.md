## About

I am a software developer at [Vector 35 Inc.](https://vector35.com), where I am one of the core developers of [Binary Ninja](https://binary.ninja/) and lead the development of its [debugger](https://github.com/Vector35/debugger), including its [Time-Travel Debugging (TTD) integration](https://docs.binary.ninja/guide/debugger/dbgeng-ttd.html). My research interests span reverse engineering, malware analysis, vulnerability research, and code obfuscation. My independent research covers security in USB devices, software updates, Windows, and virtualization. I have [11 CVEs](/cves/). My research has been covered by [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/cyber-security/lenovo-fingerprint-usb-stick-sold-as-aes-256-encrypted-had-a-plain-sd-card-inside-ai-assisted-follow-up-finds-a-backdoor-and-hardcoded-keys-in-other-secure-usb-products), [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/privacy/microsoft-ai-images-hidden-tracking-id/), and [*Security Now!*](https://www.grc.com/sn/SN-1103-Notes.pdf), among [others](/media-coverage/).

I have spoken at [BSides Portland](https://bsidespdx.org/events/2026/speakers) (2026), [RE//verse](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rv6jdnQ4YhY) (2026), and [CODEGATE](https://codegate.org/) (2025), guested on the [*Behind the Binary*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=50QiuaJ6l8M&list=PLjiTz6DAEpuLAykjYGpAUDL-tCrmTpXTf) podcast by Google Cloud Security, and have been invited to present on various technical platforms. I have published in peer-reviewed journals including the *Journal of Computer Security* and *IEEE TIFS*, co-authored a book on assembly language and reverse engineering, and created an introductory Binary Ninja course on [Open Security Training 2 (OST2)](https://ost2.fyi/Dbg1103). I am a peer reviewer for CCS, ESORICS, DSN, JCS, and other cybersecurity venues. 

I am a reviewer of [*Paged Out!*](https://pagedout.institute/) magazine, and the administrator and reviewer of [crackmes.one](https://crackmes.one). I took 1st place in the [Grand Reverse Engineering Challenge](https://binary.ninja/2021/09/02/winning-the-grand-re-challenge.html), completed the [Flare-On](https://flare-on.com/) reverse engineering challenge five consecutive times (2022–2026), and am a member of the [Shellphish](https://shellphish.net/) CTF team. I also contribute to widely-used open-source security tooling, including Google FLARE's [capa](https://github.com/mandiant/capa) (120k+ annual downloads) and [CodeLLDB](https://github.com/vadimcn/codelldb) (8.4M+ installs).
